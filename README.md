> **SUZU** — vibecoder focused on localization, tooling and reverse engineering.

I build a large part of my projects with the help of **AI coding agents**. I define the goals, direct the work, monitor the agents, review the output, test the results and decide what actually ships.

My main goal is to use these tools to make content available in **languages and regions that were previously left behind**, especially when localization would otherwise require years of specialized reverse engineering and engineering work.

The **School Days** series is what sparked that interest for me. Wanting to experience and preserve these works in my native language, **Brazilian Portuguese**, eventually became a broader interest in localization, tooling and technical research.
