I'm **SUZU**, a vibecoder focused on localization, tooling, reverse engineering and the occasional independent project. A large part of my work is developed with the help of **AI coding agents**: I use them for research, prototyping, implementation, format investigation and iteration, while I define the goals, supervise the process, review changes, test the results and decide what actually ships. One of my main objectives with this workflow is to make content available in regions and languages that historically had little access to it — especially projects that might otherwise require years of specialized reverse engineering, custom tooling, engine research and localization work. My main language focus is **Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR)**. I'm a fan of the **School Days** series, and that franchise is what originally pushed me toward localization: wanting to experience and preserve those games in my native language gradually led me into scripts, assets, game engines, modding and reverse engineering. The project I care about most is **[Days Series PT-BR](https://github.com/tsubaki-0x/Days-Series-PTBR)**, where I bring together translations, technical research and tooling for the franchise. Outside of that, I sometimes build independent experiments simply because an idea or technical problem is interesting enough to explore.
